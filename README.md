# Stay Healthy Houston

## About
This website is made to help people learn how to prevent the spread of communicable diseases. It also gives a space where people can share their own experiences with being sick and what helped them get better or deal with it.

The goal is to help our local community stay safer and more aware, even if its just small things like washing hands more or staying home when sick.

## What the Website Includes
- Blog posts about health and disease prevention  
- A podcast talking about how diseases spread  
- A simulation video showing how fast things can spread  
- A section where people can share their own experiences  
- Simple prevention tips anyone can follow  

## Why This Matters
A lot of people don't realize how easy it is for diseases to spread. This site is meant to make people think more about their actions and how they affect others around them.

Even small choices can make a big difference, like covering your mouth when coughing or avoiding others when your sick.

## Target Audience
This website is mainly for people in the Houston area, but really, anyone can use it. Its made to be easy to understand so everyone can learn something from it.

## How to Use the Site
Just go through each section using the navigation bar. You can read, listen, and watch the content to learn more about staying healthy.
