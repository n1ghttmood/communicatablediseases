Why washing your hands actually matters

Washing your hands might seem small but it really helps stop germs from spreading. A lot of people forget to do it or rush it, but taking 20 seconds can protect you and others.

Staying home when sick

If you feel sick, its better to stay home instead of going out. It helps stop others from getting infected and keeps the community safer.

Prevention Tips

  • Wash your hands often
  • Cover your mouth when coughing
  • Avoid close contact when sick
  • Get vaccines if available

These simple things might not seem like a big deal, but they really do help everyone stay safer.
